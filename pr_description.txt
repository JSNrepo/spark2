💡 What: Added semantic `<fieldset>` and `<legend>` grouping to the custom radio card inputs for "Vehicle Type" and applied `focus-within` styles to the parent wrappers.
🎯 Why: Hidden native radio inputs (`sr-only`) lose visible keyboard focus, making it inaccessible for keyboard users to identify their current selection. Without `<fieldset>`, screen readers also miss the context of the grouped inputs.
📸 Before/After: Screenshots captured visually verifying focus rings on keyboard navigation for the modified components.
♿ Accessibility: Ensures WCAG compliance for focus visibility (2.4.7) and semantic form structures (3.3.2).

Includes journal entry logged in `.Jules/palette.md` detailing the focus management pattern for custom card inputs.
