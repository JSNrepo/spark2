### What you discovered
- Breadcrumb navigation paths in `ParkingLotDetails.tsx` lack proper accessibility markup.
- The active/current page item (`{parkingLot.name}`) didn't have `aria-current="page"`.
- This missing attribute prevents screen readers from announcing which breadcrumb represents the currently viewed page.

### What you fixed/added
- Added `aria-current="page"` to the final breadcrumb item (`<span className="text-gray-900">{parkingLot.name}</span>`) in `src/pages/ParkingLot/ParkingLotDetails.tsx`.
- This change aligns with the standard accessibility pattern for breadcrumbs, making it clearer for assistive technologies to determine the current step in the user's location.
- Updated README.md with screenshots and a video of the feature.

### Next Recommended Target
- Investigate other pages utilizing similar breadcrumb patterns (e.g. `BookingPage.tsx` or similar routes) to ensure consistency in accessibility markup for active elements across the app.
